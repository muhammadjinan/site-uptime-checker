Runner Infrastructure Routing

Monitoring nodes operate via GitHub Actions on Microsoft Azure data centers. Physical execution locations are dynamically allocated based on the OS matrix:

  • US_EAST_OR_WEST (Ubuntu): Typically routes to Azure East US 2 (Virginia) or West US 2.
  • US_CENTRAL_OR_EU (Windows): Balances across Central US, East US, or West Europe.
  • GLOBAL_SECONDARY (macOS): Runs on dedicated Apple Silicon/Intel racks located in South Central US, East US, or West Europe.
